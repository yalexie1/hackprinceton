# Judge walkthrough · about 3 minutes

Open http://localhost:5173. Before presenting, install Srihaan's actual saved audits and the private building cache, then confirm the selected result says **Based on this property's records · Saved property audit**. The app rejects example-input results. A saved audit replays an existing analysis; it does not calculate a new one.

1. **Choose a property.** Start with 5836 La Mirada Ave for a homeowner audience, or Culver Connect for a commercial audience. The cards explain how much building detail was measured. Show a real side-angle photo or the actual scan where available. The interactive welcome house is an educational model.
2. **Set preferences.** Use the preselected recorded budget and role for the saved audit. Explain that new calculations can use a different budget and twelve months of utility bills, when the actual data and credentials are available.
3. **Read the plan.** Lead with yearly energy-bill savings and cost to get started. Open one included upgrade, then compare an option needing more budget. Explain why other findings were excluded. Technical return calculations and source records are available in expandable details.
4. **Check the evidence.** Open Solar & roof to show the actual aerial image. Explain which measurements are direct and which still need checking. Estimates without utility-bill calibration or contractor quotes remain estimates.
5. **Show the next step.** Open Documents to review the available draft types. Enter only verified party and project details. Required missing details are highlighted as needing confirmation; no name, price or legal term is invented to complete a form.

If actual records are missing, the app explains what is unavailable. Do not claim the generic house matches a property's geometry, that a saved replay is a fresh analysis, or that an unsigned draft is a completed legal review. Record observed glitches and judge feedback on Message Board #1.
