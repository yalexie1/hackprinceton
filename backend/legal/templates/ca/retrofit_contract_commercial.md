Owner states that Owner is the {{owner_role_text}} of the Property.

**Construction lender.** Name: {{construction_lender_name}}. Address: {{construction_lender_address}}. This space is required in a commercial construction contract and is printed whether or not there is a construction lender; a blank means Owner has stated none.

**16.2 Property.** The work will be done at {{property_address}}, {{property_city}}, California ("the Property"), a {{property_use_type}} property. Legal description: {{legal_description}}.

**16.3 What this contract is for.** Owner wants to reduce the energy use and energy cost of the building at the Property. Contractor will install the energy improvements listed in **Schedule A**. The improvements were selected with the help of an energy analysis of the Property, summarised in **Schedule C**. That analysis is owner-furnished information. It is not engineered design, it is not part of Contractor's promises, and no saving shown in it is guaranteed by this contract unless a separate Energy Savings Guarantee is signed.

**16.4 Contract documents and order of precedence.** The contract documents are this contract and Schedules A, B, C, D and E. If they conflict, this contract governs over the schedules, and a later written change order governs over all of them. Schedule C is non-binding and never governs.

### Payment
<!-- clause: CA-COM-04, CA-PAY-01, CA-PAY-06 -->

**16.14 Progress payments.** Owner will pay Contractor in the stages listed in **Schedule B**. Each payment is due only when the work the stage describes is done.

**16.15 Payment requests and timing.** Contractor will send Owner a written payment demand naming the stage completed. Owner will pay the undisputed amount **within 30 days** after receiving that demand, unless Schedule B states a different period in writing, in which case Schedule B governs. If Owner disputes in good faith that a stage is complete, Owner will pay the undisputed part, may withhold no more than **150 per cent** of the disputed amount, and will tell Contractor in writing what is disputed and why.

**16.16 Late payment.** An undisputed amount not paid when due carries a penalty of **2 per cent per month** in place of interest, and the prevailing party in an action to collect it is entitled to its attorney's fees.

**16.17 Disputed work notified as complete.** Where Contractor notifies Owner that disputed work is complete, Owner will accept or reject it in writing within 10 days, and will pay the retention related to accepted work within 10 days of that acceptance.

**16.18 Suspension.** If Owner fails to pay an undisputed amount within 30 days after it is due and does not cure within 10 days of Contractor's written notice, Contractor may suspend the work until it is paid, and the completion date is extended by the length of the suspension.

### Retention
<!-- clause: CA-COM-05, CA-COM-06, CA-PAY-02, CA-PAY-03, CA-PAY-04, CA-PAY-05, CA-PAY-07 -->

**16.19 Retention cap.** Owner may withhold retention of no more than **{{retention_percent}} per cent** of each progress payment and no more than that percentage of the Contract Price. For a contract entered on or after 1 January 2026 that percentage may not exceed **5**. Retention withheld by a construction lender under a construction loan agreement is not retention under this clause.

**16.20 Retention release.** Owner will pay the retention to Contractor **within 45 days after completion** of the work. Owner may withhold from that payment no more than 150 per cent of an amount disputed in good faith. Retention not paid when due carries a penalty of **2 per cent per month** in place of interest, and the prevailing party in an action to collect it is entitled to its attorney's fees. Neither party may waive these retention rights in advance.

### Subcontractors
<!-- clause: CA-COM-07, CA-PAY-05, CA-PAY-08 -->

**16.21 Payment to subcontractors.** Contractor will pay each subcontractor its share of each progress payment **within 7 days** after Contractor receives that payment, unless Contractor and that subcontractor have agreed otherwise in writing. Contractor may withhold no more than 150 per cent of an amount disputed in good faith. A late payment carries a penalty of 2 per cent per month.

**16.22 Retention flow-down.** Retention withheld by Contractor from a subcontractor, and by a subcontractor from a sub-subcontractor, may not exceed the percentage withheld by Owner from Contractor, and in no case the cap stated in 16.19. Contractor will pay each subcontractor its share of a retention payment **within 10 days** after receiving it.

### Liens and Notices
<!-- clause: CA-COM-09, CA-LIEN-01, CA-LIEN-02, CA-LIEN-03, CA-LIEN-04, CA-LIEN-05, CA-LIEN-06, CA-LIEN-07 -->

**16.23 Preliminary notices.** Contractor will tell each subcontractor and material supplier that a preliminary notice must be given within 20 days of first furnishing labour or materials in order to preserve a mechanics lien claim, and will give Owner a copy of each preliminary notice Contractor receives.

**16.24 Waivers and releases.** With each payment, Contractor will give Owner a waiver and release on the statutory form for the work covered by that payment, and will obtain the same from each subcontractor and supplier whose work is covered. Owner may withhold the next payment until those releases are received. A waiver of lien rights outside the statutory forms is void.

**16.25 Notice of completion.** Owner may record a notice of completion within 15 days after completion. If Owner records one, Owner will send a copy **within 10 days** of recording, by registered or certified mail, to Contractor and to every person who gave Owner a preliminary notice. The exemption from that mailing duty for residential property of four units or fewer does not apply to this contract.

### Indemnity and Insurance
<!-- clause: CA-COM-08, CA-IND-01, CA-IND-02, CA-IND-03 -->

**16.26 Indemnity.** Contractor will indemnify and defend Owner against claims for bodily injury, death or damage to property to the extent caused by the negligence or wilful misconduct of Contractor or anyone for whom Contractor is responsible. This duty does not extend to any claim arising from Owner's sole negligence, Owner's wilful misconduct, Owner's active negligence, or defects in design furnished by Owner. Nothing in this contract requires either party to indemnify the other against liability for the other's own sole negligence or wilful misconduct, and any provision that purported to do so is void.

**16.27 Insurance.** Contractor will carry the insurance listed in **Schedule D** for the periods stated there, and will name Owner as an additional insured on the commercial general liability policy to the extent of Contractor's indemnity obligation in 16.26.
