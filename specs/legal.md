# Legal documents: templates, clause library, placeholders

Owner: @Sri-SriPod (agent lane). Code: `backend/legal/`. Statutory research: `specs/legal/california.md` and `specs/legal/texas.md` (owner @yalexie1).

Status: **draft v0.1, 2026-11-14.** Document 1 (California residential retrofit contract) is written in full. Documents 2 to 6 are listed with their purpose only and come next.

> **Every generated document is a draft for attorney review, not legal advice.** Nothing in this file is legal advice either. The statute summaries below were read from unofficial mirrors of the California codes because the official site refused automated access (see the verification log). Before any document is signed, a California-licensed attorney must check it against the official text.

## 1. How this file is used

1. The agent's legal subagent receives a `DocumentRequest` (`doc_type`, `state`, `measure_ids`, user-supplied `fields`) plus the run's `Plan` and `PropertySummary`.
2. `backend/legal/` picks the template for `(doc_type, state)` from this file, resolves every `{{placeholder}}` from the placeholder table (section 5), and evaluates every statutory check (section 6).
3. It returns a `DocumentDraft`: the filled `markdown`, `unfilled_placeholders` (every placeholder it could not fill from a listed source), `statutory_checks`, and `disclaimer` (section 1.2).

### 1.1 Drafting rules (binding on the code and on any LLM step)

- **Never invent a fact.** If a party name, licence number, address, legal description, price, date or insurance detail is not in a listed source, leave a visible blank of the form `[[BLANK: placeholder_name, what is needed]]` and add the name to `unfilled_placeholders`. Do not guess, do not use example values, do not carry values across runs.
- **Statutory text is inserted, never written.** Placeholders whose name starts with `stat_` hold wording the statute prescribes word for word (for example the mechanics lien warning). Their only source is the verbatim text in `specs/legal/california.md`. If that file has no verbatim text for a `stat_` placeholder, the draft shows `[[BLANK: stat_..., insert verbatim statutory text]]` and the matching statutory check is `satisfied=false`. An LLM may not paraphrase, shorten, translate or reorder `stat_` text.
- **Clause text in section 4 is our own wording.** It was written for this project and is not copied from any published form. The LLM may fill placeholders in it but must not rewrite it, except where a clause is marked *[editable]*.
- **Estimates are not prices.** Analysis outputs (`FinancialBreakdown.installed_cost_usd`, savings, payback) are estimates. They never fill the contract price, the down payment or a progress payment. Those come from the contractor's bid (user-supplied fields). Estimates may appear only in Schedule C, which is labelled as non-binding.
- **Formatting is part of compliance.** Type size and bold rules (section 6) are carried as markup hints: `<!-- fmt: 12pt bold -->` before the element. The Word/PDF export (P2) turns them into real formatting. The Markdown preview shows them as a styled block. Until export exists, every check that depends on type size is reported `satisfied=false` with the note "type size enforced only in export".

### 1.2 Disclaimer string

Used verbatim as `DocumentDraft.disclaimer` and printed at the top and bottom of every draft:

> DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW. NOT LEGAL ADVICE. This document was generated by software from an energy analysis and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Do not sign it until a lawyer licensed in {{state_name}} has reviewed and completed it.

## 3. Document 1, California: purpose and routing

**Purpose.** A home improvement contract between an owner (or tenant) of a California dwelling and a licensed contractor, covering the energy measures the plan selected, written to meet Business and Professions Code §7159 and the related sections.

**When it applies.** `doc_type = retrofit_contract_residential`, `state = "CA"`, `PropertySummary.segment = "residential"`. B&P §7159 covers home improvement contracts whose aggregate price exceeds $500 (§7159(b)). Below that, or for a §7159.10 service-and-repair contract ($750 or less, buyer-initiated, paid on completion), the generator still produces this contract but marks the check `CA-R-00` with a note that a simpler form may be lawful.

**Measure routing.**
- `PV_ROOF` is **excluded** from this contract. Residential solar needs the §7169 disclosure and goes in document 3. If `measure_ids` contains `PV_ROOF`, drop it from the scope, add a warning to the draft ("Solar is covered by a separate solar installation agreement"), and record check `CA-R-21`.
- `LAYOUT` (desk placement) is not construction and is dropped silently.
- Every other `MeasureId` in `measure_ids` that is also in `Plan.selected` becomes one line of the scope (Schedule A). A requested measure that is not in `Plan.selected` is still included, with a warning that it is outside the budget plan.

**Financing.** If the user says the work depends on a loan or on financing arranged or referred by the contractor (`financing_contingent = yes`), clause 4.13 is included (B&P §7163). Residential PACE is out of scope for this document and gets a warning: it is governed by separate California PACE statutes that have not been reviewed here.

### 4.B Generated tables

**Progress payment table** (`progress_payment_table`, also Schedule B). Columns: stage number, work or delivery completed, amount in dollars and cents.
- Default stages: one per measure in Schedule A, in the order of `Plan.selected` (rank), plus a final stage "Final inspections passed and hand-over documents delivered" holding at least 10% of the price.
- Amounts: from `stage_amounts_usd` if the user gave them. Otherwise the generator proposes amounts by splitting (`contract_price_usd` − `down_payment_usd`) in proportion to each measure's `finance.installed_cost_usd.expected`, rounds to cents, puts the rounding difference in the final stage, and marks every row *proposed by software, confirm with contractor*.
- Checks: amounts sum exactly to the price less the down payment (`CA-R-09`); no stage pays for work before it is done (each row's text names a completed item, never a date alone) (`CA-R-10`).

**Schedule A rows** (`schedule_a_rows`). From each `MeasureResult` for the routed measures: improvement = `title`; location = rooms named by `anchors[].room_id` / `label`; work = `description`; materials and equipment = rendered from `params` (for example insulation R-value, heat pump efficiency rating, lamp count) and from `contractor_equipment_specs[measure_id]` if the user supplied it; completion test = the measure's default in the table below. Flags with `status` `likely_upgrade` or `blocked` (for example "electrical.service") are listed under the row as "Known risks that may need a change order", with their `added_cost_usd` range.

| Measure | Default completion test |
|---|---|
| `ENV_ATTIC` | insulation installed to the stated R-value over the stated area; depth markers visible |
| `ENV_WALL` | wall cavities filled; holes patched and finished to match |
| `ENV_AIRSEAL` | sealing done at the listed locations; a blower-door test after the work reports the result |
| `WIN_REPLACE` | units installed, flashed and sealed; labels showing rated performance left on until inspection |

## 7. California facts this design depends on

Short form. The authoritative statements belong in `specs/legal/california.md`. Items marked **(differs from HANDOFF.md 7)** correct the first-pass notes.

- B&P §7159 currently shows its last amendment as Stats. 2020, ch. 312 (SB 1474), effective 2021-01-01, which added the five-day senior right. **(differs from HANDOFF.md 7, which says "last amended effective 2026-01-01")**
- §7159 lists the required items in (c), (d) and (e) but prescribes no order for them, only placement: first page (c)(3)(B), bond notice near signatures (c)(6), cancel notice next to the buyer's signature (e)(6).
- Bond exemption, §7159.5(a)(8): a contractor with an approved performance and payment bond (or equivalent or joint control) is exempt from the down-payment cap, the progress-payment rules and the mechanics lien warning.
- Civ. Code §1632 (translation of contracts negotiated in Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese or Korean) **does not cover** home improvement contracts under B&P §§7151.2 and 7159 (§1632(g)(2)). The language duty for this contract comes instead from Civ. Code §1689.7(a)(4). **(not in HANDOFF.md 7)**
- Civ. Code §8811 (5% retention cap, contracts from 2026-01-01) **does not apply** to a residential project that is not mixed-use and is four stories or fewer (§8811(b)(3)). **(HANDOFF.md 7 lists it without this exception)**
- Civ. Code §8800: 30 days to pay after a payment request applies "unless otherwise agreed in writing", so clause 4.7 may set its own period.
- Civ. Code §2782(c)(3): the active-negligence rule for private owners does not apply to a homeowner improving their own single-family home; §2782(a) (sole negligence, wilful misconduct) still applies.
- B&P §7125 (workers' compensation certificate on file) is a licensing rule, not contract content, and is in force until 2028-01-01. **(HANDOFF.md 7 says it could not be retrieved)**
- Civ. Code §1689.7(a)(1) and (c) (10-point statement, Notice of Cancellation form) **exclude** contracts under B&P §§7151.2 and 7159.10. For a home improvement contract the duties come from §1689.7(a)(4) (language, 12-point bold statement next to the signature), §1689.7(b) (first-page details, no exclusion) and B&P §7159(e)(6) (title, notice, form). The title "Three-Day Right to Cancel" is in §7159(e)(6), not in §1689.7.
- Civ. Code §1689.6(a)(2): for a §7151.2 contract the cancellation period runs from the buyer's **receipt of a signed and dated copy**, not from signing.
- Civ. Code §1689.13 emergency waiver: a separate statement, dated and signed by the buyer, describing the emergency and expressly waiving the right to cancel. It does **not** require the buyer's handwriting or "own words". **(differs from HANDOFF.md 7)**
- B&P §7163(a)(3): the federal rescission condition applies "if applicable".
